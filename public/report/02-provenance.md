## 2. Scope, retained inputs and version history

This is a self-contained audit of the retained calculation, not the full chat archive or a purported recovery of all 57 hosted files or 1,891 websites. All source rows, rebuilt datasets, derived results, code and source register used here are embedded. The exact attached spreadsheet and codebook can be extracted from this file. Their SHA-256 hashes and byte counts are in the manifest. External source links are for verification; opening this report and reading its calculations does not require a network connection.

ARPD is Michael K. Miller’s historical ruling-party dataset. The supplied codebook is October 2019, Version 1.0. Its original sample definition is broader than our outcome: a party may be the supreme ruler or a significant preeminent vehicle of a regime. Breakdowns include democratization, distinct-party transfer, nonparty rule and major organizational breaks. It is not a preexisting uniformly coded measure of decisive CCP-like supremacy.
