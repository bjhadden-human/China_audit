## 1. Answer and audit conclusion

The frozen China calculation is reproducible: 61.383367% eventual loss with continuous Mozambique, or 63.254945% with split-2004. “Approximately 60:40” is coarse rounding, not a third estimate, an average of scenarios, or an additional synthesis. No weights were supplied for combining branches.

The documentary-corrected implementation excludes the added Nicaragua-2012 episode and Kazakhstan source row 243. Under the same bridge assumptions, its outputs are 61.792648% and 63.660565%. These are conditional model results, not statistically calibrated national predictions.

The sources support mechanisms and directional judgments, but do not estimate the 0.75 China multiplier or the 50-year hazard half-life. Computational reconstruction is closed; empirical identification of these assumptions is not claimed. Finite historical observation cannot identify an infinite future without tail assumptions: this report proves the distinction with equal-likelihood constructions rather than treating it as a missing citation.

## 3. Operational rules and documentary adjudication

1. Keep party organization, personal ruler, ruling coalition and state identity distinct. Personnel continuity or a shared label is insufficient by itself.

2. Count central-state ruling spells, not decades of subordinate provincial control duplicated alongside a union-level spell. Territorial division or reunification does not itself imply loss by a continuing central organization.

3. For the central proxy, examine a dominant governing apparatus, not a requirement that every leader be subordinate to an autonomous party bureaucracy. Legal monopoly, meaningful electoral contestability and executive turnover remain different endpoint conventions.

4. Require affirmative evidence for adding an authoritarian-supremacy episode. Institutional dominance alone does not settle authoritarian eligibility. Exclusion of an unestablished added episode is not a claim that authoritarianism was absent.

5. Do not cherry-pick a threshold for one case and describe all heterogeneous source endpoints as interchangeable. Keep alternative operationalizations named, rather than assigning invented probabilities to them.

Nicaragua is a substantive correction: the contemporaneous report describes institutional dominance but also explicitly classifies the country as an electoral democracy. A strict-evidence version therefore drops the added 2012 onset. Kazakhstan is both a wording correction and a unit/organization correction: rows 245–247 were omitted, but row 243 remained; September 1991 transformation preceded independent-state inception in December. These facts justify a separately coded exclusion, not an invented long organizational continuation.

## 4. Historical survival model and estimation

Observation ends in 2015; exit coordinate 2016 means right censoring at the end of that year range. For original records, a numeric last year becomes exit=last+1. Reviewed endpoints supplied directly to the code are exit coordinates: exit=1990 is not an assertion that the entire calendar year 1990 contributed exposure. Boundary choices are annual-to-continuous approximations, not exact event dates.

```text
t_i = exit_i - start_i
entry_i = max(1940 - start_i, 0)
d_i = 1 for an observed loss, 0 for censoring
h(a) = alpha * exp(beta*a)
log L = sum_i { d_i*(log(alpha)+beta*t_i)
                - integral(entry_i,t_i) h(a) da }
alpha_hat(beta) = D / sum_i integral(entry_i,t_i) exp(beta*a) da
AIC = 2*p + 2*NLL
P(loss by age A+u | survival to A) = 1-exp[-integral(A,A+u) h(a) da]
P(eventual loss | A) = 1, if beta >= 0
                     1-exp[h(A)/beta], if beta < 0
```

The likelihood conditions on survival to delayed entry; pre-1940 person-years are not treated as observed exposure. The implementation profiles alpha analytically and solves the beta score by Brent root finding, with stable series and log-sum-exp calculations. Strata use the literal source accession text containing Revolution and the source Marxist indicator; ideological intuition does not override those filters.

Country-block bootstrap accounts for repeated spells within normalized countries, not arbitrary worldwide shock dependence. Cross-national heterogeneity, shared calendar shocks, cohort effects, unobserved frailty and changes in institutional technology can generate an apparent declining age hazard. It is not automatically learning or durability improvement within the same regime. A pooled historical fit is not a validated China regression.

## 5. Frozen China bridge, arithmetic and thresholds

```text
t = years after assessment; reference age A = 77
h0 = m * exp(log_alpha + beta*77)
k = ln(2) / half_life
h_China(t) = h0 * exp(-k*t)
R_infinity = h0/k
P_loss(T) = 1-exp[-R_infinity*(1-exp(-k*T))]
P_eventual_loss = 1-exp(-R_infinity)
P_indefinite_retention = exp(-R_infinity)
central: m=0.75; half_life=50 years
```

The initial multiplier lowers the historical age-77 hazard by 25%. The 50-year half-life is slower hazard decline than the historical broad Gompertz decline, offsetting some of that discount. These are frozen elicited assumptions, not fitted China coefficients. Neither number is quantitatively estimated by the sources below. There is no scenario-count weighting, separate war-probability add-on, or independent multiplier for each correlated pressure.

The Frozen saved rows use exactly the six-decimal historical coefficients prescribed in the supplied methods. Frozen refit uses full-precision coefficients. Corrected refit uses the changed records and full precision. Tiny rounding differences are not coding effects. The 18 saved eventual probabilities and 54 saved 10/25/50-year probabilities were compared with the user-supplied grid and match at the numerical precision used. Split-2009 is historical sensitivity, not an invented third frozen China baseline.

## 6. China evidence: support, counterevidence and dependence

Organizational/coercive reach and effective surveillance support a durability discount directionally. They do not identify 0.75. The published AI-tocracy abstract establishes a local procurement–unrest result and complementary innovation mechanism; it does not measure national regime-collapse effects or prove indefinite control.

[[web:2304]] [[web:2427]] [[web:1332]]

Centralization may enhance coordinated control while increasing information distortion and local-policy mismatch. Laboratories of Autocracy is a working paper about policy and economic outcomes, not a coefficient translating those outcomes to loss of CCP supremacy. IMF identifies resilience alongside major structural and balance-sheet pressures; projected GDP is not realized GDP, and weak growth is not mechanically political failure.

[[web:2317]] [[web:1052]]

Purges can strengthen obedience while damaging experience, initiative and candid advice. CSIS presents differing conditional interpretations. Brookings’ October preview and the Monitor’s subsequent report occur at different information dates; Zhang Shengmin’s promotion is not a contradiction created by reading the preview as a final personnel report. CSIS and Brookings share contributors, and Monitor quotes a Brookings author. Count underlying observations and arguments, not institutional logos as independent samples.

[[web:2291]] [[web:2296]] [[web:2293]]

The Nathan Law essay is perspective and mechanism discussion, not measured survival evidence. Opacity, protests or elite turnover alone are not numerical risk increments. Domestic and international mechanisms can interact without coordinated actors; correlated fiscal, economic, informational and succession pressures must not be counted repeatedly.

[[web:1544]]

The evidence therefore supports a structured comparison of forces but does not empirically calibrate the frozen parameters. The reconstructed probability is conditional on them. This is a positive provenance finding—not a suggestion to replace the specified formula with an additional informal synthesis.

## 8. Alternative hazard families and old-age diagnostics

```text
Exponential: h(a)=rate; eventual loss=1 for positive rate
Weibull: h(a)=k*a^(k-1)/scale^k; eventual loss=1 for k>0
Cure mixture: S(a)=c+(1-c)*exp(-rate*a)
  conditional eventual loss at A = 1-c/S(A)
Power-declining: h(a)=a0*(1+a/tau)^(-p)
  eventual loss=1 if p<=1; finite remaining hazard if p>1
```

Families use the same delayed-entry likelihood within each dataset. The code retains bounds, starting points, selected optimizer success and bound-contact flags. All 72 selected alternative fits report success; success is not proof of a unique/global optimum. Revolutionary power-declining fits contact a bound. AIC cannot validate the unobserved tail merely by close observed fit.

Equal-likelihood tail construction: retain the fitted hazard through the maximum observed age L, then set h(a)=h(L)*exp[-k(a-L)] for a>L. No observation exits beyond L, so changing k beyond that point cannot alter any observed event density or exposure integral. Choose k to achieve target remaining hazard. Broad L=102 admits 40% or 80% eventual-loss constructions; narrow L=72 admits 25% or 75%. In narrow strata the conditioning age 77 is already beyond observed support.

The original script wrote the likelihood-difference column as a constant zero. Here observed likelihood is recomputed; differences are numerical roundoff, with maximum magnitude below 10^-10. Target probabilities are achieved to numerical tolerance. These are constructed counterexamples, not estimated tails or probabilities assigned to worlds.

A persistent strictly positive hazard floor makes the infinite integral diverge, hence eventual loss=1 by assumption. An exponentially decaying hazard has a finite integral and a point mass on indefinite retention. No finite historical sample can nonparametrically distinguish these infinite futures. More citations or bootstrap replications cannot close that identification problem. It is resolved here by showing exactly which assumption determines the conclusion, not by inventing evidence about an unobserved infinite future.

## 11. Justified boundaries and final assessment

Three boundaries cannot responsibly be erased:

1. Original archive lineage: repeated fetches of the user-hosted Netlify directory and individual files returned no content or HTTP diagnostics in this interface, though another system reportedly succeeded. The archive is not declared missing. Exact recovery of unavailable original file bytes or chat wording cannot be fabricated. Required numerical material was instead regenerated from the exact two attached binaries and supplied code/settings; saved bridge values were matched. This closes computational dependencies without falsely claiming ingestion of all 57 files. The original browsing list and full chat are outside C, not silently recovered.

2. Future identification: the equal-likelihood constructions prove why an infinite-horizon target cannot be identified from this finite observation window without a tail restriction. Replacing this finding with a point estimate presented as data-only would damage the audit.

3. Historical scope: the original comparator window ends in 2015. Selectively appending later famous failures while leaving other spells censored would introduce outcome-selected updating. A complete post-2015 extension is a separate dataset project, not an honest patch to this frozen reconstruction. No result here claims to be an updated global comparator panel through 2026.

There is also a resolved measurement finding: the ARPD-derived proxy pools different organizations and thresholds. The 15 consequential adjudications identify the rules and evidence, the corrected track removes two insufficiently supported records, and all supplied alternative scenarios are recomputed. This is not represented as a newly independently sourced reclassification of every retained historical spell into a single perfectly homogeneous CCP-like outcome.

Final assessment: the approximately 60:40 statement has a reproducible conditional derivation. The two evidence-driven exclusions do not overturn its direction. Much larger uncertainty comes from the hazard family, the unobserved tail, transfer from historical party spells to China, and the unestimated China parameters. Neither the corrected coding range nor the bootstrap justifies claiming a calibrated 60% probability. The audit preserves the calculation while making its evidentiary status exact. It is not a new weighted synthesis.
